Privacy

HNx is a lightweight Hacker News reader built to stay out of your way — and out of your data.

What we collect

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No analytics or ads. No trackers, pixels, or marketing SDKs.
  • No third-party scripts in your browser. Fonts and libraries are self-hosted.
  • No persistent preferences. Theme and feed choices stay in memory for the session and are not written to cookies or local storage.

What your browser talks to

For story and comment data, the page only requests this site’s own origin (/api/*). There is no client-side call to Google Fonts, Firebase, Algolia, or other third-party APIs from the browser under normal use.

Our servers fetch public Hacker News data from upstream providers (the official HN Firebase API and the Algolia HN Search API for full threads) so the browser does not need a direct relationship with those hosts.

What the host may see

Like any website, the hosting provider (Vercel) processes standard request metadata such as IP address, user agent, URL path, and timestamps for delivery and abuse prevention. HNx does not add product analytics on top of that.

What leaves when you open a story

Links to external articles open on the destination site. Those sites (and any trackers they embed) are outside HNx’s control. Outbound links use rel="noopener noreferrer" and a strict referrer policy so we do not leak this site’s path as a referrer.

Security measures

  • Content-Security-Policy locked to first-party assets and APIs
  • Strict-Transport-Security, frame denial, nosniff, Permissions-Policy
  • HTML from HN sanitized with a vendored DOMPurify build before render
  • No open URL-fetch proxy endpoints

Source

This is a small, inspectable codebase. If something here is wrong or could be stricter, that is a welcome bug report.
